Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

What HR templates does PeopleForge offer? +

PeopleForge has five tools live right now, all free to use with no account: a custom performance evaluation form builder, a career ladder framework builder, a recruiting framework builder, an engagement survey builder, and PeopleForge Studio for custom-built people systems. Each generates a finished document from your answers, which you can copy and edit. More products are in development.

How do I get access to the tools? +

The four live tools are free and need no account or payment — open one, answer the questions, copy the document it produces. For PeopleForge Studio, our custom build service, you either book a discovery call or fill in the intake form and we come back with a scope and a quote.

How much do PeopleForge HR templates cost? +

The five tools currently live are free. Listed prices for products still in development are indicative and nothing is purchasable on the site yet. PeopleForge Studio, our custom build service, starts at $7,500 and is quoted per project after a discovery call.

What format are the templates delivered in? +

All templates are delivered as instant downloads in both PDF and Notion format. Some frameworks also include Google Sheets or Excel versions where relevant (e.g. compensation models, career ladder scorecards). You get lifetime access and all future updates are free.

Do you offer consulting for companies that need custom HR systems? +

Yes. PeopleForge offers HR consulting ranging from a 90-minute audit session ($500) to full fractional CHRO retainers (from $2,000/month). We build custom compensation systems, performance frameworks, handbooks, and onboarding processes tailored to your company. Book a free discovery call to discuss your needs.
