AI playbooks

How to run an Acuolo playbook

An Acuolo playbook is a file you give to your AI assistant. It turns ChatGPT or Claude into an experienced People & Culture partner that runs one process with you — building a career ladder, running a feedback round, or deciding a pay review — step by step, for your company. There is nothing to install, and no account with us.

An AI assistantChatGPT or Claude. Free plans work to get started; paid plans give you more room.
The playbook fileA .md file you receive after buying. You can open it in any text editor.
30–45 minutesOnce, for the setup interview. After that, each task starts in seconds.

1. Load the playbook

Use a Project where you can: it keeps the playbook loaded for every conversation, so you set it up once.

ChatGPT

  1. In the sidebar, create a new Project — for example "People processes".
  2. Open the Project and add the playbook file to its files.
  3. Start a new chat inside the Project.

Claude

  1. Go to Projects and create a new one.
  2. Add the playbook file to the Project's knowledge.
  3. Start a new chat inside the Project.

No Projects?

  1. Open a new conversation in any AI assistant.
  2. Upload the file, or open it in a text editor and paste the whole text in.
  3. Keep that conversation for this process — or paste the file again next time.

2. Type Start and answer the setup questions

  1. The AI interviews you about your company — one or two questions at a time, each with a suggested answer you can simply accept. It asks things like your levels, your teams, who decides what, and how you want things written.
  2. Have these to hand if you can (none are required): your job titles, any job descriptions or existing ladder, your company values, and — for Pay Progression — your own salary data. The AI never invents salaries or market data.
  3. It confirms each part back to you in a sentence, so nothing is assumed.

3. Save your company profile — the step people miss

AI assistants don't remember between conversations. At the end of setup, the AI gives you a company profile block. Copy it into a file — for example company-profile.md — and add it to the same Project. From then on, every conversation knows your company, and you never answer the setup questions twice.
  • Using more than one playbook? Put all of them in the same Project, and keep one profile file with every playbook's profile block in it. They share your levels and competencies, and it keeps you within the file limits of free plans.
  • Things change. When the AI re-issues the profile (or, for Pay Progression, the people list and decision register), replace the file with the new version.

Start each task with a command. A few to begin with — every playbook lists all of its commands near the top.

Good habits

  • One case, one conversation. Start a new chat for each person's review or each feedback round. Long conversations make any AI less reliable.
  • Read before you send. The AI drafts every message and report; you check it and send it.
  • The AI recommends, you decide. Every playbook is built that way. Decisions about people stay with your company.
  • If it drifts, say so: "Please follow the playbook — section 5." It will correct itself.

Your data

  • Share only what the task needs. Initials or roles often work as well as full names.
  • Follow your company's rules on using AI tools with employee information.
  • Check your AI provider's data settings. Personal plans usually let you control whether your chats are used to train models; business plans are generally designed for company data.
  • Nothing goes to Acuolo. The playbook runs entirely in your own AI account.

Questions

Do I need a paid ChatGPT or Claude plan?

No. Free plans are enough to get started, but they limit how many files you can add and how much you can use the assistant each day. Paid plans give more room. If your plan has no Projects, paste the playbook into a single conversation instead.

Why does the AI ask the setup questions again?

Because it doesn't remember between conversations. Add your saved company profile file to the Project, or paste it at the start of the conversation.

The file won't upload. What now?

Open it in any text editor (it is plain text), copy everything, and paste it into the conversation. It works the same way.

Can I use it in another language?

Yes. Tell the AI your language and tone during setup, and it writes everything in it.

Can I change the playbook?

Yes, freely, for use inside your own company. The licence at the end of each file explains what you can't do (mainly: resell or share it outside your company).

Can it connect to our HR system?

Not on its own. With Acuolo Studio, the same playbooks run in "connected mode" — reading your records and filing reports in your own systems, always with your approval before anything is sent.
